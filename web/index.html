Know how you drive and what your car really costs

DriverBalance turns the driving journal you export from the Volvo Cars app into clear statistics: trips, fuel use, what each trip cost, DPF health, oil intervals, maintenance and the full cost of owning your car.

Sign in with Google

Invite-only for now. Google sign-in is used only to recognise you: DriverBalance never sees your Google password.

Trips and habits

Distance, driving time, when you drive, your longest journeys and a driving calendar.

Fuel

Consumption per trip and over time, fuel burned per active day, cold starts and short trips.

Cost of every trip

An approximate cost for each trip: fuel at the local price plus a fair share of insurance, service and tyres, weighted by driving time and distance.

DPF health

For diesels: whether the particulate filter gets the long drives it needs, with a reminder when it is overdue.

Oil and maintenance

Every service visit with the work and parts, oil change intervals side by side, tyre sets and swaps.

Costs

Fuel, service, insurance, tolls and more, cost per km, and purchase, financing and resale in one cost of ownership.

Ask your assistant

Connect a chat assistant over MCP to log fuel and receipts as they happen, or ask what your last trip cost.

Your data stays yours

Only you see your data. Download it or delete your account at any time. No ads, nothing sold.

How it works

  1. Export the driving journal (CSV) from the Volvo Cars app.
  2. Import it into DriverBalance.
  3. Add fuel, service and other costs as they happen. Not ready yet? Try a demo car with made-up data first.

DriverBalance is a personal, non-commercial project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Volvo Cars.
